01

先把问题说清楚Start with clarity

每款产品只承诺它真正解决的问题，版本和平台状态如实呈现。Each product states the problem it actually solves, with honest platform and release status.

02

尊重每个平台Respect each platform

从系统能力和使用习惯出发，让快捷入口、通知与界面都像平台本来的一部分。We work with native capabilities and conventions so every interaction feels at home.

能留在设备上的数据就尽量留在设备上；需要联网或同步时，把边界说清楚。Data stays on device when it can. When a service or sync is needed, the boundary is made clear.
