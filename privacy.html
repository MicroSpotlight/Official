官网隐私说明Website privacy notice

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 14 日Updated: August 14, 2026

MicroSpotlight 官方网站是一个静态产品介绍站点。我们不会通过本网站要求你注册账号，也没有接入广告、分析、行为追踪或营销 Cookie。The MicroSpotlight website is a static product directory. It does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or marketing cookies.

网站会处理什么What the website processes

当你访问本网站时，托管服务和网络基础设施可能按其正常运行方式处理 IP 地址、请求时间、页面路径、浏览器类型与安全日志。MicroSpotlight 官网本身不会把这些信息写入自有分析系统。When you visit, the hosting provider and network infrastructure may process IP addresses, request times, page paths, browser types, and security logs as part of normal operation. The MicroSpotlight site does not send this information to an analytics system of its own.

语言偏好Language preference

当你切换网站语言时，浏览器会在本机保存一个语言偏好。它不会发送给 MicroSpotlight，也不会用于识别你。When you switch languages, the site stores that preference locally in your browser. It is not sent to MicroSpotlight and is not used to identify you.

外部链接与产品External links and products

本网站会链接到各产品官网、应用商店与 GitHub。各产品处理数据的方式可能不同，请以对应产品官网中的隐私政策为准。This site links to product websites, app stores, and GitHub. Each product may process data differently; refer to the privacy policy on that product's website.
